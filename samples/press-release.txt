Gloo, the Leading Technology Platform for the Faith Ecosystem, Announces Record Revenue Growth

BOULDER, CO — Gloo, a technology platform serving the faith ecosystem, today announced strong Q3 results, with total revenue of $32.6M, representing year-over-year growth of 432%. The company also reported improved EBITDA margins and a clear path to profitability.

"We had an exceptional quarter of execution," said Scott Beck, CEO of Gloo. "Our platform continues to gain traction with enterprise customers, and we are expanding our capabilities to address a broader set of needs within the faith ecosystem. With our values-aligned AI capabilities and growing customer base, we are well-positioned for continued growth."

Key Highlights:
- Total revenue of $32.6M, up 432% year-over-year
- Annual Contract Value (ACV) increased 3x
- Over 600 paying customers across products and services
- Launched new values-aligned AI capabilities
- Completed two accretive acquisitions expanding platform capabilities
- On track for profitability in FY2025

Gloo's technology platform connects churches, ministries, and faith-based organizations with the tools and solutions they need to grow their impact. The company serves the faith ecosystem through its suite of products and services, including its Community Flourishing Lens (CFL) and National Church Pulse (NCP) offerings.

About Gloo
Gloo is a technology platform for the faith ecosystem. The company provides solutions that help faith-based organizations connect, grow, and serve their communities. Headquartered in Boulder, Colorado, Gloo serves over 140,000 organizations across the United States.
